# Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026.

1. What we access. After the owner authorizes it, the app can upload videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It reads no one else's data.

2. What we store. TikTok access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app keeps no database of users, viewers, or followers.

3. What we collect from others. Nothing. The app does not collect, analyze, sell, or share personal information about anyone other than the owner.

4. Third parties. The app sends the owner's videos only to TikTok, through its official API. It sends data to no one else.

5. Retention and deletion. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok settings under Security & Permissions, and can delete stored tokens by deleting the local files.

6. Children. The app is not directed at children and collects no data about anyone.

7. Changes. Any changes will appear on this page with a new date.

8. Contact. airframelegends@gmail.com
